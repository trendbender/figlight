figlight

A tiny lightbox that keeps your captions. Click any photo below.

Keyboard: Esc closes, ← and → move between images. On a phone, swipe. The caption under the enlarged photo is the page’s own <figcaption>, links and credits included.

What it does not do

  • No dependencies, no build step, no network calls of its own.
  • Small icons are skipped: this one stays put.
  • An image wrapped in a link stays a link.

How it was switched on here

One line, nothing else:

<script src="../dist/figlight.js"></script>
